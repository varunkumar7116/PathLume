# PathLume Visual Mapping & Alignment Guide

This document details the physical scan procedure for creating visual feature maps aligned with SITE WORLD 3D GLB models.

---

## 1. Physical Scanning Guidelines

1. **Lighting Conditions**: Scan venue under standard operational lighting (avoid extreme glare or darkness).
2. **Feature-Rich Routes**: Focus scanner on textured walls, signage, structural columns, and entrance doors.
3. **Loop Closure**: Walk in closed continuous loops across corridors to minimize drift.

---

## 2. SITE WORLD Alignment Matrix

Map coordinates must be transformed to match canonical SITE WORLD:
- **Units**: 1 unit = 1.0 meter
- **Axes**: `+Y` Up, `+X` Right, `-Z` North/Forward
- **Origin**: Aligned to designated building entrance landmark (`0,0,0`)
