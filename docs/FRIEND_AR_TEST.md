# PATHLUME — Friend AR Test Instructions

Thank you for testing the PATHLUME AR Navigation prototype! Please follow these simple instructions to test ARCore tracking and 3D spatial marker stability on your Android device.

---

## Steps to Test

1. **Install the APK** on your ARCore-supported Android phone.
2. **Open PATHLUME**.
3. **Allow camera permission** when prompted.
4. Press **START AR TEST**.
5. **Slowly move the phone around the room** so ARCore can scan surfaces and detect features.
6. Wait until the **Tracking** status changes to **TRACKING**.
7. Press **PLACE MARKER** to anchor a 3D cyan cube in front of you.
8. **Slowly walk or move around** the room while keeping the phone aimed towards the room.
9. **Look back at the marker**.
10. **Check whether the marker stays in approximately the same physical location** in the real world.
11. Press **RESET** and repeat if necessary.

---

## What to Send Back

- **If ARCore says unavailable / unsupported:** Please send a screenshot.
- **If tracking does not start:** Please send a screenshot showing the status panel.
- **If the marker jumps or drifts excessively:** Record a short video (5-10 seconds) if possible.
- **If everything works:** Send a screenshot showing **Tracking: TRACKING** and the placed 3D marker in your room.

Thank you!
